Get resources, build a house, don't die. Axe fells trees for wood, pickaxe chips stone (it keeps giving until it's gone), shovel digs dirt. Right-click an empty tile to build with what you've gathered; right-click a door, shutter or trapdoor to open, close or bar it — bars only go on from inside. Ten arrows to start; misses land where they land, and a hit gives one back more often than not. Zombies take three sword hits, climb ladders, and every touch might infect you.
