A timber house with shutters, a barred door and a trapdoor to the loft. Shutters are open (see-through, climbable) or closed (opaque, breakable) or barred. Doors and shutters can only be barred from inside; the trapdoor only from above. Zombies take three sword hits, climb ladders, and every hit they land on you carries a chance of infection you will not be told about. Anything that sees you calls the others.
