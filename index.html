Get resources, build a house, don't die. Axe fells trees for logs (one per trunk block; some trees are two wide), pickaxe chips stone (it keeps giving until it's gone), shovel digs dirt — dirt, leaves and logs are placeable straight off the ground. Right-click an empty tile → Craft… saws a log into four planks, or turns planks or stone into a wall, floor, ladder, door, shutter or trapdoor; it lands on your hotbar (or in your pack) as a real item. Select it and it follows your cursor as a ghost — click to place, pick another slot to stop. Dismantle anything you built with the matching tool and you get the whole thing back, ready to place again; zombies breaking it down leaves nothing. Right-click a door, shutter or trapdoor to open, close or bar it — bars only go on from inside. Ten arrows to start; misses land where they land, and a hit gives one back more often than not. Zombies take three sword hits, climb ladders, scramble slowly over two-block obstacles, chew through walls if that's what's in the way, and every touch might infect you.
